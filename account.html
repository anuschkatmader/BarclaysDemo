Soft opt-in vs explicit consent — OneTrust setup

  • Collection point: "Account Opening - Soft Opt-In" (this form). The newsletter pop-up on the homepage is a separate collection point with explicit consent.
  • Purpose "Offers on products similar to yours": implicit consent ON, lifespan 24 months, "show explicit consent checkbox in preference centre" ON. Lawful basis: legitimate interests / PECR reg. 22 soft opt-in.
  • In the form: the purpose is a hidden field, default Checked, so submitting records implicit consent → ACTIVE without the customer ticking anything.
  • Refusal at collection: ticking "I don't want to receive offers…" unticks that hidden field before submit, so the purpose isn't granted.
  • No double opt-in on this form — nothing to confirm; the customer is already verified. The newsletter can use double opt-in (PENDING until confirmed).
  • Show next: the data subject's receipt in OneTrust, then Marketing preferences → confirm explicitly or opt out (OPT_OUT).
